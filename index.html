🌸On Your Special Day🌸

My Dearest Love,

How do I put into words what you mean to me? You are the quiet morning and the brilliant sunset. You are the warm cup of tea on a cold day, the laugh that sneaks up on me when I least expect it, the hand I reach for without thinking.

On this day that the world gave you to us, I want you to know — truly know — that every version of my life is better because you exist in it.

You have this rare gift of making ordinary moments feel like magic. A walk. A meal. A glance across a crowded room. With you, everything is softer, brighter, more alive.

So today, on your birthday, I don't just celebrate the day you were born — I celebrate every moment since the universe decided our paths should cross. I celebrate the person you are. The way you make me want to be better. The warmth you carry everywhere.

Happy Birthday, my love. May this year be as luminous and extraordinary as you are.

Forever and always yours,
With all my love 💕
